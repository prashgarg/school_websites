Topic correlation matrix

Pearson correlation across crawled schools (log-scaled scores)

Across-school correlations of the six focal topic intensities. Positive values (red) mean the two topics tend to co-occur on the same school's website; negative values (blue) mean they trade off. The diagonal is 1 by construction. Sector filter applies.

Six focal topics, one panel each. Maps share the same color scale within each topic but are not comparable across topics — scores are mean phrase-matches per page and differ in baseline level by topic.

Private-school sample: PSS 2021–22 universe, crawled May 2026. Topic scores: mean phrase matches per page, averaged across crawled pages per school, then averaged across schools in each geography. The six focal topics shown here are the focal subset of a 22-topic keyword ontology — see Methods for construction details and the Paper for the joint regression headline numbers.
