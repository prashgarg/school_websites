What U.S. K–12 schools tell families on their websites, measured at national scale.

The U.S. K–12 market enrolls 50 million students with no required disclosure of price or program. We measure 81,109 public, private, and charter school websites — what they emphasize, how that varies with local market structure, and what it predicts for enrollment growth and school closure.
