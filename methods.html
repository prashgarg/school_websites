Methods

Measurement layer overview. Full specification, dictionaries, and identification discussion are in the paper; this page is the one-screen summary.

Universe and corpus

We start from the union of NCES’s Common Core of Data (CCD, 2022–23) and Private School Survey (PSS, 2021–22): 145,569 schools across all 50 states. Of these, 122,415 received a discovered URL and 114,624 returned a live homepage at crawl time. The April–May 2026 crawl successfully retrieved page text for 81,109 schools, producing 1,835,152 unique pages after within-school deduplication (mean 23 pages/school). The 33,515-school gap from active-seeded to crawled is dominated by Cloudflare/anti-bot blocks, JavaScript-only sites, and soft-404 stubs beyond the homepage.

Measurement

Every variable in the main analysis is computed from page metadata, deterministic page-type rules, or keyword dictionaries. Four feature families:

  1. Structural — pages crawled, crawl depth, word count, soft-404 flags.
  2. Page-type presence — admissions, tuition, mission, academics, athletics, arts, policy, etc., from URL/heading rules.
  3. Transparency signals — tuition-amount regex, financial-aid mention, accreditation mention, test-scores listing.
  4. Keyword topics — 22 topic-family dictionaries with prominence weighting (homepage ×3, heading ×2, body ×1, deeper-page ×0.5).

Why deterministic

Replicable from a fixed dictionary, costless to extend to historical Internet Archive snapshots, verifiable by external researchers. A complementary two-stage LLM ontology measurement on a 2,958-school validation subsample appears as an appendix; the two layers converge on religious identity and tuition transparency but diverge on family-partnership and academic-rigor — documented transparently.

Outcomes

Enrollment growth: PSS biennial waves 2013–14 through 2021–22 (private, 38,692 school-years) and CCD Membership 2016–17 through 2022–23 (public, 251,792 school-years).

Closure (private): state Department of Education enrollment rosters for California, New York, Pennsylvania, Illinois, Ohio, and Wisconsin (1,553 matched schools; 70 closure events). Closure is defined as permanent disappearance from the state roster, persisting in subsequent years. We are extending these panels through 2024–25 and adding additional states.

Closure (public): national CCD presence-disappearance over seven waves 2016–17 through 2022–23. 109,204 schools, 9,344 closures in the full panel; 320 events in the within-Nonsectarian regression sample.
