Methodology

From 145,569 Schools to Six Topic Scores

Four stages: identify the school universe, find each school's URL, crawl the website, classify the text. Each stage is described below.

Stage 1 — School Universe

Two NCES administrative datasets, merged on NCES school ID, form the school spine. Both cover academic year 2021–22.

P

Private School Survey (PSS)

33,000 private schools. Provides name, address, affiliation (Catholic / Non-Catholic religious / Nonsectarian), grade span, enrollment, PSS-reported website, and geographic coordinates. We retain all schools with a valid NCES ID.

C

Common Core of Data (CCD)

90,000+ public schools including charter and magnet schools. We merge with Local Education Agency (LEA) records to use district domains as fallback URL seeds for schools without their own site.

Stage 2 — URL Discovery

Finding the correct official URL for 145,000 schools required six sequential sources, applied in order of precision.

1

Administrative records

PSS and CCD both report self-declared website URLs where available. This covers ~60% of public schools and ~25% of private schools directly.

2

District-sibling inference

For public schools missing a URL, we infer subdomain or sub-directory patterns from the district domain and school name, adding ~7,000 schools at effectively zero cost.

3

OpenStreetMap & WikiData

Overpass API and SPARQL queries retrieve URLs for ~4,000 additional schools, primarily rural private schools and charter networks.

4

Catholic diocesan directories

Diocese-level directories and NCEA data add ~3,500 Catholic private schools not captured by earlier sources.

5

IRS Form 990

ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer provides website URLs from 990 filings for ~700 additional private schools organized as 501(c)(3) nonprofits.

6

Wayback Machine CDX

Internet Archive CDX API provides historical URLs for ~5,000 schools whose current sites are offline but whose archived content remains crawlable.

Stage 3 — Web Crawl

We crawl each school's website starting from its seed URL, following links within the same domain for up to 20 unique pages. The crawler (Python, Playwright + requests) records page URL, title, visible text, structural features (forms, iframes), and outbound social-media links. External domains and PDF files are excluded from the text corpus, though PDF presence is noted.

The national crawl ran in May 2026 across five cloud VMs (shards 0–4), each processing a non-overlapping slice of the school spine. Total: 421,663 pages across 80,620 schools in approximately 48 hours. We cap crawl depth at three hops from the homepage to avoid deep-linking into student portals or administrative systems.

Exclusions

  • Domains returning HTTP 4xx/5xx for all crawl attempts
  • Sites behind bot-detection (Cloudflare, Imperva) at crawl time
  • District homepages mistakenly assigned to individual schools (identified by URL-to-school-name mismatch)

Stage 4 — Topic Classification

We apply a 23-topic regex ontology to each page's text. For each topic–page pair, we count the number of matched phrases. A school's topic score is the mean match count per page, averaged across all unique crawled pages — controlling for variation in how many pages a school has. We also construct binary presence indicators and page-count variables; all three representations are in the public dataset.

The ontology was built iteratively: we started with a conceptual vocabulary for each topic, ran it against a random 1,000-school sample, manually reviewed false positives, and refined patterns until precision was adequate. Topics with low discriminant validity (high cross-topic correlation) were merged or dropped. The six focal topics below survived this process with the lowest inter-topic correlations and strongest outcome associations.

Outcome and context data

Enrollment
PSS enrollment panels
Six PSS waves (2007–08 to 2021–22). Log enrollment growth between consecutive waves is the primary outcome variable.
Closure
State DOE records
Six states: CA, IL, NY, OH, PA, WI. 2018–2025. A school is coded closed if it appears in earlier PSS waves but not confirmed active in later state records.
Competition
PSS coordinates
Log count of same-sector private schools within 10 km, constructed via k-d tree on PSS lat/lon coordinates. N ≈ 14,000 with valid coordinates.
Achievement
SEDA v4.1
Stanford Education Data Archive. School-level scores for 73,795 public schools; county-level scores for private-school analysis. Merged via NCES ID and county FIPS.
Politics
MIT Election Lab 2020
County-level 2020 presidential vote totals. Republican share computed as GOP votes / total votes. Merged via 5-digit county FIPS.
Social media
Crawl extractor
Platform presence (Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, Twitter, TikTok, LinkedIn) identified from outbound social-media links detected during the crawl.
